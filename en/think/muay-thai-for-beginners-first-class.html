COMPARE · FIRST CLASS

Your first Muay Thai class. No surprises.

Most people who don't start aren't short on motivation. They just don't know what's going to happen in that first hour, what to bring, whether they'll get hit. Here's all of it, so you arrive knowing exactly what to expect.

See the first hour What to bring

With me there's no beginner class with twelve people. There's a session with you, and the first one is an assessment: how you move, what you're after, and pads so you take something home.

MINUTE BY MINUTE

This is the first hour with me.

BEFORE

One message

You message me on WhatsApp: name, experience (none, relax), what you're after. We fix a day and a place. No forms, no joining fee.

MIN 0 TO 10

We talk and warm up

Old injuries, goal, any fears. A gentle warm-up that lets me see how you move.

MIN 10 TO 35

Stance, feet and two strikes

How to stand, how to move your feet, and the jab and the cross. Slowly. I correct every rep. Nobody else is watching.

MIN 35 TO 52

Pads

Short rounds with what you just learned. This is what makes you want to come back. I put the gloves on you if you've never done it.

MIN 52 TO 60

Calm and honesty

What you did well, what we'd work on, and whether this is for you. You pay at the end, €30, cash or Bizum. No pressure to continue.

WHAT TO BRING

The complete list. It's short.

Don't buy anything before the first session. Everything you need, I provide.

  • Training clothes. Shorts or leggings, a t-shirt. We train barefoot, so trainers are only for getting there.
  • Water. A big bottle. You'll sweat more than you think.
  • A small towel. Optional but appreciated.
  • Gloves, wraps, shin guards, pads. I provide them. If later you want your own, I'll tell you which and where, no fashion brands or things you don't need.
  • No mouthguard or groin guard on day one. There's no contact. When there is, if there is, I'll tell you well in advance.
USUAL NERVES

Four things you're worried about that you shouldn't be.

Will I get hit?

No. We work technique and pads. There's no sparring until you ask and I can see you're ready. And even then, controlled.

Will I look stupid?

In front of whom? A coach who's seen ten thousand first jabs. Yours won't surprise me.

Am I too out of shape?

The first session adapts to where you are today. Fitness is the result of training, not the requirement.

What if I don't like it?

You'll have spent €30 and an hour finding out, and I'll tell you too if I think it's not a fit. No pack, no contract, no pressure.

QUESTIONS

Your first Muay Thai class, from scratch.

What should I bring to my first Muay Thai class?

Training clothes, water and a bit of nerve. Gloves, wraps and pads are on me. Don't buy anything beforehand.

Do I have to spar on the first day?

No. Not the first or the tenth if you don't want to. Sparring is optional: if you want it, we do it; if not, we don't.

How long is a session and how much does it cost?

60 minutes, €30. Pay at the end. No joining fee, no contract.

Which gloves should I buy when I want my own?

For pads and bag, 12 or 14 oz from a serious brand without overpaying. I'll tell you which when the time comes. Fit matters more than the brand.

I've never trained. Can I start?

Yes. The first class is an assessment: we see where you're starting from and build from there. Sparring is optional; if you'd rather not, we work only on technique, balance and cardio, and I bring the gloves, pads and shin guards.

Now you know what to expect. Book the hour.

€30, gear included, no obligation to continue. One message and we fix a day and a place.
