Brazilian by birth. Coach by obsession.

I'm Dennys. Over 15 years on the mats and in the ring, over 10 years teaching people how to actually use what they learn. Purple belt in Jiu-Jitsu, corner work for UFC and Rizin fighters, and now based in Torre del Mar, travelling to Vélez-Málaga, Nerja, Rincón de la Victoria and Málaga.

A pretty technique that doesn't hold up under pressure isn't worth much.

My job isn't to teach you a highlight reel of techniques. It's to build someone who understands distance, timing, footwork, balance, and what to do when the plan falls apart mid-exchange, because it always does.

WHAT I TEACH

Three ways to train. One way of teaching.

Muay Thai

The base of most of my work. Strikes, clinch, footwork and the timing that makes it all mean something.

MMA

Striking and grappling stop being separate worlds. We work the transitions: takedowns, takedown defence and some ground work.

Self-defence

No secret techniques. Awareness, distance, decisions under stress, and knowing when the smartest move is to leave. Always one-on-one.
