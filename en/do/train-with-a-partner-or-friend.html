WHO IT'S FOR

Three ways to come as a pair.

01

Couples

A plan that isn't dinner. I teach you both the same thing, each at your own pace, and you leave with something in common nobody else has. And yes, you can hold pads for each other.

02

Friends

Starting alone feels daunting. With a friend it's the excuse you were missing. And healthy competition means neither of you skips.

03

Clubmates

You both train and want to sharpen specific things. We work as a pair with correction for both, and you take drills back to the club.

HOW IT WORKS

What changes compared with a solo session.

Different levels, no problem

I adapt the drills to each of you within the same session. The more experienced one doesn't get bored; the beginner doesn't get lost.

You hold pads for each other

Learning to hold pads is learning to read a strike. It's a skill you take home to keep going.

The price is per session

Not per person. It works out cheaper than two solo sessions and I confirm it in writing before you book.

As a gift

A session for two works very well as a present. Message me and I'll prepare something you can hand over.

QUESTIONS

Questions about training as a pair.

Can we train together with different levels?

Yes. It's the norm. I adapt the drills to each of you within the same session and teach you to hold pads for each other.

Is the price per person or per session?

Per session, for both of you. I confirm it in writing before you book. It works out cheaper than two solo sessions.

Does it work for self-defence?

Yes, and it's one of the best formats. Many defence drills work better with a second person.

Can three of us come?

Two maximum. With three I can't correct everyone the way I want to, and that's what you're paying for.

Do we need to bring anything?

Training clothes and water. Gloves and pads for both of you are on me.

Up for it? Ask for the price.
