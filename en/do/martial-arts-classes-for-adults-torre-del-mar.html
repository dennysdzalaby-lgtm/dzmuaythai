QUESTIONS

Questions before booking.

Which martial art do you teach exactly?

Muay Thai, MMA and self-defence. For now I don't teach Jiu-Jitsu, because I don't have a mat, nor karate, taekwondo, judo or krav maga. If you're looking for one of those, I'm telling you now so you don't waste time.

What's the best martial art for an adult beginner?

For most people, Muay Thai: fitness, clear technique and a base for self-defence. If you'd rather not take strikes, Muay Thai without sparring: technique, balance and cardio. If you want the complete sport, MMA. We decide in the first session.

How much experience do I need?

None. The first session is an assessment adapted to where you are today.

How much do martial arts classes for adults cost?

€30 for a 60-minute session, €270 for the 10-lesson pack. Same rate for all three disciplines. No joining fee.
