THE PROGRAMME

What we work on, session by session.

Ten sessions is a complete block. You'll progress with fewer too, but this is how it's designed.

SESSION 1

Assessment and the avoidance layer

How you move, what worries you. Reading a situation heating up, how to leave without looking like fleeing, what to do with your hands and voice.

SESSIONS 2 TO 4

Distance and calm

A guard that doesn't look aggressive, footwork so nobody can reach you, breathing when the body locks up. This is where Muay Thai comes in.

SESSIONS 5 TO 8

Physical response

Simple strikes that work when tired and scared: knee, elbow, low kick. How to escape a wrist grab, a neck grab, and a bear hug from behind.

SESSIONS 9 TO 10

Under pressure

Scenarios with real but controlled stress. It's the only way to know if what you learned comes out on its own. And a review of when not to use any of it.

HONESTLY

What this programme doesn't promise.

  • That you'll win any fight. Nobody can promise that. The goal is getting home, not winning.
  • Secret techniques. Everything I teach is simple on purpose. Complicated doesn't come out under stress.
  • Results without repetition. One session gives you information. Ten give you reflexes. The difference is repetition.
QUESTIONS

Questions before booking.

What does the self-defence course cover?

Three layers: avoiding and reading situations, distance and calm, and simple physical response (strikes, escaping grabs). Plus pressure work at the end. It doesn't cover weapons or techniques that take years to master.

Is it for women?

For women and men. I adapt the scenarios to what worries you. Many of my female students come for this. There's a dedicated page on Muay Thai for women. Self-defence for women →

Does it work for people over 50?

Yes. The avoidance and distance layers have no age. The physical response is built on simple strikes without gymnast flexibility.

How much is a private lesson?

A 60-minute private lesson costs €30 per person, with gloves, pads and shin guards included, and you pay at the end. Two of you in the same session: €45, €22.50 each; if there are more of you, message me and we'll work out the price. The 10-lesson pack is €270, paid when you take it.

Where are the lessons?

On the beach, at your home or in a park near you. I'm based in Torre del Mar and travel to Vélez-Málaga, Nerja, Rincón de la Victoria and Málaga. We fix the exact spot on WhatsApp when you book.

Can I start if I've never trained?

Yes. The first class is an assessment and we build from wherever you are; fitness comes with training. What your first class looks like →

Do you work with companies or teams?

Yes, for small groups from the same workplace (hospitality, healthcare, security). Message me and we'll talk.
